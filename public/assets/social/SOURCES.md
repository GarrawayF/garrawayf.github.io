# Social brand assets

Downloaded 2026-09-12. Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners.

- `facebook.png`: Primary logo from the [Facebook brand asset pack](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/facebook/logo/).
- `instagram.svg`: White glyph from the [Instagram brand asset pack](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/instagram/instagram-brand/), displayed over a gradient in CSS.
- `line.png`: Brand icon from [LINE's official logo resources](https://www.line.me/en/logo).
- `x.svg`: Logo from the [X brand toolkit](https://about.x.com/en/who-we-are/brand-toolkit).
- `note.png`: [note's official app icon](https://assets.st-note.com/poc-image/manual/note-common-images/production/icons/android-chrome-192x192.png), linked from note.com.

All assets are served locally; icon rendering does not depend on an external CDN or social feed request.
